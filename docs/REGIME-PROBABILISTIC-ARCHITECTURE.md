## 1. Purpose & Philosophical Framing

### 1.1 Core Research Objective
Phase 3.9 extends BLACKBOX X from unconditional Monte Carlo simulation into **regime-conditioned probabilistic research**.

The primary quantitative research question answered by this engine is:

> **"How does the simulated distribution of portfolio outcomes change when the simulation is conditioned on observed market regimes and empirically observed regime transitions?"**

### 1.2 Non-Predictive Charter & Conceptual Delineation
BLACKBOX X **does not predict future market regimes**. 

The engine explicitly separates and maintains four distinct analytical concepts:
1. **Historical Observation**: Rule-based regime classifications applied to historical daily price bars in the offline demonstration dataset.
2. **Empirical Transition Estimation**: The observed transition counts $N_{ij}$ and frequencies $P_{ij}$ between consecutive daily historical regime states.
3. **Simulated Regime Path**: A stochastic sequence of simulated regime states $R_0 \to R_1 \to \dots \to R_H$ generated by a discrete-time Markov chain governed strictly by empirical transition frequencies.
4. **Simulated Portfolio Outcome**: The resulting wealth trajectory and drawdown distribution generated by sampling returns conditioned on the simulated regime path.

## 3. Market Regime Model & Classification Source

### 3.1 Existing Regime Taxonomy
The platform defines four discrete macro regimes $\mathcal{R}$:
1. **`BULL`**: Trending positive growth environment (rolling 60-day annualized return $> 5.0\%$, rolling annualized volatility $\le 35.0\%$).
2. **`BEAR`**: Sustained negative or stagnant growth (rolling 60-day annualized return $\le 5.0\%$, rolling annualized volatility $\le 35.0\%$).
3. **`HIGH_VOL`**: Elevated volatility regime (rolling 60-day annualized volatility $> 35.0\%$, supersedes return direction).
4. **`LOW_VOL`**: Subdued volatility regime (rolling 60-day annualized volatility $< 15.0\%$).

### 3.2 Classification Source & Invariants
- **Source of Truth**: Daily regime labels are determined by evaluating the synchronized portfolio equity trajectory using `detectRegimes(prices, 60)` from Phase 2 / Phase 3.7 (`src/core/portfolio/portfolioRegimes.ts`).
- **Data Period**: Spans the 1,825 synchronized daily return intervals ($t \in [1, 1825]$) from `2019-01-01` to `2023-12-31`.
- **Classification Status**: Labels are strictly **historical post-hoc classifications** of the offline demonstration dataset. They are **never** predictive indicators or forward forecasts.

## 4. Regime Observation Matrix & Chronological Alignment

### 4.1 Chronological Sequence Alignment
Let $t \in \{1, \dots, T\}$ be the index of synchronized daily observations ($T = 1,825$). Each observation row contains:
$$\mathbf{O}_t = \Big( \text{date}_t, \; R_t, \; \mathbf{r}_t, \; R_{p,t} \Big)$$
where:
- $\text{date}_t$: Calendar date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- $R_t \in \mathcal{R}$: Classified regime label at day $t$.
- $\mathbf{r}_t = [r_{\text{GOLD},t}, r_{\text{BTC},t}, r_{\text{NVDA},t}]^T \in \mathbb{R}^3$: Synchronized asset return vector.
- $R_{p,t} = \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{r}_t$: Realized portfolio return.

### 4.2 Data Integrity Rules
1. **Zero Forward-Fill**: Missing dates or skipped sessions are strictly prohibited.
2. **Zero Synthetic Label Substitution**: Regime labels trace directly to empirical rolling calculations; no synthetic or smoothed labels may be injected.
3. **Strict Contemporaneous Intersection**: Every observation contains authentic contemporaneous data across all three assets and the corresponding regime state.

## 5. Empirical Regime Transition Matrix

### 5.1 Transition Counting & Frequency Formulation
For all consecutive observation pairs $(R_t, R_{t+1})$ where $t \in \{1, \dots, T-1\}$:
1. **Transition Count Matrix** $\mathbf{N} \in \mathbb{N}^{4 \times 4}$:
   $$N_{ij} = \sum_{t=1}^{T-1} \mathbb{I}\Big( R_t = i \;\land\; R_{t+1} = j \Big) \quad \forall i, j \in \mathcal{R}$$

2. **Total Outgoing Transitions**:
   $$N_i = \sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}} N_{ij}$$

3. **Empirical Transition Probability Matrix** $\mathbf{P} \in [0, 1]^{4 \times 4}$:
   $$P_{ij} = \begin{cases} \frac{N_{ij}}{N_i}, & N_i > 0 \\ 0, & N_i = 0 \end{cases}$$

### 5.2 Row-Stochastic Invariant
For every originating regime $i$ where $N_i > 0$:
$$\sum_{j \in \mathcal{R}} P_{ij} = 1.0$$

```
│                 EMPIRICAL TRANSITION MATRIX TOPOLOGY                        │
├─────────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┤
│ Originating (i) │  ► BULL      │  ► BEAR      │  ► HIGH_VOL  │  ► LOW_VOL   │
├─────────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
│ BULL            │   P_BULL,BULL│   P_BULL,BEAR│   P_BULL,HVOL│   P_BULL,LVOL│
│ BEAR            │   P_BEAR,BULL│   P_BEAR,BEAR│   P_BEAR,HVOL│   P_BEAR,LVOL│
│ HIGH_VOL        │   P_HVOL,BULL│   P_HVOL,BEAR│   P_HVOL,HVOL│   P_HVOL,LVOL│

└─────────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

*Mandatory Disclaimer*: The transition matrix $\mathbf{P}$ reflects empirical transition frequencies observed in the offline simulated demonstration dataset. It does not represent an external stochastic prediction of real-world financial transitions.

## 6. Zero-Data & Sparse Regime Policy

### 6.1 Thresholds & Failure Handling
To prevent statistical distortion and numerical instability:
- **Minimum Observation Threshold**:
  $$\text{MIN\_REGIME\_OBSERVATIONS} = 20$$

  `"SPARSE REGIME: Limited observation count (n < 20). Empirical parameters may exhibit elevated sample variance."`

### 6.2 Zero-Transition Absorption Prevention
If an originating regime $i$ has $N_i = 0$ outgoing transitions:
1. The regime transition distribution is marked as `'INSUFFICIENT_TRANSITION_DATA'`.
2. The simulation engine is **strictly blocked** from silently defaulting or fabricating probabilities.
3. The UI renders the transition row as unavailable (`N/A`), and AI tools return an explicit validation error:  
   `"Cannot execute regime-conditioned Monte Carlo: Regime [i] has zero observed outgoing transitions."`
4. **Prohibition of Arbitrary Smoothing**: Laplace smoothing (+1 pseudocounts) or arbitrary Gaussian blurring is **strictly forbidden** unless explicitly requested by the user and documented as an artificial smoothing policy.

## 7. Regime-Conditioned Return Distributions & Joint Vectors

### 7.1 Conditional Data Partitioning
For each regime $r \in \mathcal{R}$:
$$\mathcal{D}_r = \Big\{ \mathbf{r}_t \;\Big|\; t \in \{1, \dots, T\} \;\land\; R_t = r \Big\}$$
Let $T_r = |\mathcal{D}_r|$ be the sample size of regime $r$.

### 7.2 Conditional Moment Estimation
1. **Conditional Mean Return Vector** $\boldsymbol{\mu}_r \in \mathbb{R}^3$:
   $$\boldsymbol{\mu}_r = \frac{1}{T_r} \sum_{t \in \mathcal{D}_r} \mathbf{r}_t$$
2. **Conditional Daily Covariance Matrix** $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_r \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$:
   $$\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_r = \frac{1}{T_r - 1} \sum_{t \in \mathcal{D}_r} (\mathbf{r}_t - \boldsymbol{\mu}_r)(\mathbf{r}_t - \boldsymbol{\mu}_r)^T$$

### 7.3 Multi-Asset Joint Vector Preservation Invariant
- **Zero Asset Decoupling**: For every day $t \in \mathcal{D}_r$, the return vector $\mathbf{r}_t = [r_{\text{GOLD},t}, r_{\text{BTC},t}, r_{\text{NVDA},t}]^T$ is treated as an indivisible atomic tuple.
- **Rationale**: Asset decoupling would destroy cross-asset flight-to-safety dynamics (e.g. Gold outperformance during Bitcoin/equity selloffs during `HIGH_VOL` or `BEAR` regimes).

## 8. Simulation Method A: Regime-Conditioned Bootstrap (`REGIME_BOOTSTRAP`)

### 8.1 Algorithmic Execution
For each simulated path $m \in \{1, \dots, M\}$:
1. **Initialize State**: Set day $\tau = 0$, wealth $V_{m,0} = V_0$, regime $R_{m,0} = R_{\text{start}}$.
2. **Forward Step Loop** for $\tau = 1 \dots H$:
   - **Step 2a (Return Sampling)**: Draw a discrete uniform random index $k$ from the subset index set of the active regime $R_{m,\tau-1}$:
     $$k \sim \mathcal{U}\{1, T_{R_{m,\tau-1}}\}$$
     Select the synchronized joint vector:
     $$\mathbf{r}^*_{m,\tau} = \mathbf{r}_k \in \mathcal{D}_{R_{m,\tau-1}}$$
   - **Step 2b (Portfolio Update)**: Update position values, apply bankruptcy clamping ($r^* \le -100\% \to V_{i,\tau} = 0$), compute portfolio return $R_{p,m,\tau}$, and execute rebalancing if triggered.
   - **Step 2c (Regime Transition)**: Sample the next regime state $R_{m,\tau}$ according to the cumulative transition probabilities of the current row $\mathbf{P}_{R_{m,\tau-1}, \cdot}$:
     Draw $u \sim \mathcal{U}[0, 1)$ from PRNG.
     Find regime $j$ such that $\sum_{l=1}^{j-1} P_{i,l} \le u < \sum_{l=1}^j P_{i,l}$.
     Set $R_{m,\tau} = j$.

## 9. Simulation Method B: Regime-Conditioned Parametric (`REGIME_PARAMETRIC`)

### 9.1 Algorithmic Execution
1. **Pre-computation**:
   For each regime $r \in \mathcal{R}$:
   - Estimate conditional mean $\boldsymbol{\mu}_r$ and covariance $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_r$.
   - Perform reactive Cholesky decomposition:
     $$\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_r = \mathbf{L}_r \mathbf{L}_r^T$$
   - If any regime covariance fails positive-definiteness ($L_{j,j}^2 \le 10^{-10}$), apply reactive Tikhonov diagonal regularization ($\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{r,\text{stable}} = \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_r + \lambda_r \mathbf{I}$). Log `stabilizationApplied[r] = true`.
2. **Forward Step Loop** for $\tau = 1 \dots H$:
   - **Step 2a (Correlated Shock Generation)**:
     Draw $\mathbf{z}_{m,\tau} \sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{I})$ using deterministic Box-Muller transform.
     Compute correlated shock: $\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_{m,\tau} = \mathbf{L}_{R_{m,\tau-1}} \mathbf{z}_{m,\tau}$.
     Simulated return: $\mathbf{r}^*_{m,\tau} = \boldsymbol{\mu}_{R_{m,\tau-1}} + \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_{m,\tau}$.
   - **Step 2b (Portfolio Accounting)**: Execute portfolio path update and rebalancing friction.
   - **Step 2c (Regime Transition)**: Transition regime state via empirical transition matrix $\mathbf{P}$.

## 11. Regime Persistence & Dwell Time Diagnostics

### 11.1 Empirical Metrics
1. **Regime Occupancy Frequency**:
   $$\pi_i = \frac{T_i}{T} \in [0, 1], \quad \sum_{i=1}^4 \pi_i = 1.0$$
2. **Historical Observed Dwell Time**:
   - Average duration in consecutive days: $\bar{d}_i = \frac{1}{K_i} \sum_{k=1}^{K_i} d_{i,k}$ (where $K_i$ is number of distinct regime bouts).
   - Median duration in consecutive days: $\text{Median}(d_i)$.
3. **Self-Transition Probability (Stickiness)**:
   $$P_{ii} = \frac{N_{ii}}{N_i}$$
4. **Theoretical Geometric Dwell Time** (derived from Markov chain):
   $$\mathbb{E}[D_i] = \frac{1}{1 - P_{ii}} \quad (\text{valid for } P_{ii} < 1.0)$$
   *(Note: This represents the theoretical mean duration if transitions were memoryless geometric trials).*
5. **Switching Frequency**:
   $$\text{Rate}_{\text{switch}} = \frac{\sum_{i \ne j} N_{ij}}{T - 1}$$

## 14. Regime-Conditioned Euler Risk Decomposition

For each regime subset $\mathcal{D}_r$, the engine calculates the conditional Euler risk decomposition:
1. **Marginal Risk Contribution**:
   $$\text{MRC}_{i,r} = \frac{(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_r \mathbf{w})_i}{\sigma_{p,r}}$$
2. **Component Risk Contribution**:
   $$\text{CRC}_{i,r} = w_i \times \text{MRC}_{i,r}$$
3. **Percentage Risk Contribution**:
   $$\text{PRC}_{i,r} = \frac{\text{CRC}_{i,r}}{\sigma_{p,r}}$$

*Euler Identity Invariant*:
$$\sum_{i=1}^3 \text{CRC}_{i,r} = \sigma_{p,r}, \quad \sum_{i=1}^3 \text{PRC}_{i,r} = 1.0 \quad (\forall r \in \mathcal{R})$$

This quantifies how asset risk dominance shifts across macro regimes (e.g. Bitcoin PRC expanding from $55\%$ in `LOW_VOL` to $72\%$ in `HIGH_VOL`).

## 19. Ghost Mode Insight Categories (Phase 3.1 Extension)

Ghost Mode will support five new deterministic regime insight categories:
1. `REGIME_OUTCOME_DIVERGENCE`: Triggered when median terminal wealth differs by $> 20\%$ between starting in `BULL` versus `BEAR`.

## 22. Edge Cases & Defensive Handling

1. **Dominated Dataset**: If one regime represents $100\%$ of observations, transition matrix degenerates to an absorbing state ($P_{ii} = 1.0$), matching unconditional simulation.
2. **Extreme Volatility Blowups ($r^* \le -100\%$)**:
   - Asset capital clamps to zero ($V_{i,\tau} = 0$). Total portfolio wealth $V_\tau = \sum V_{i,\tau} \ge 0$ is strictly guaranteed non-negative.
3. **Short Horizon ($H < 63$)**: Annualized CAGR and Sharpe ratios are suppressed or flagged with an institutional note.
4. **Single-Path Edge Case ($M = 1$)**: Percentiles collapse cleanly ($P_{05} = P_{50} = P_{95} = X_1$).

## 23. Institutional Limitations & Disclaimers

The specification enforces the following disclosures:
1. **Offline Demonstration Data**: Metrics and regime labels are derived from the existing offline simulated demonstration dataset (`2019-01-01` to `2023-12-31`). No real historical market feeds are claimed.
2. **Empirical Frequency Baseline**: The regime transition matrix reflects historical transition counts over a finite observation window. It is not an econometric forecast of future regime shifts.
3. **First-Order Markov Assumption**: The model assumes transitions depend only on the current regime state, abstracting away higher-order memory effects or macro cycle phases.
4. **Non-Predictive Research Tool**: Regime-conditioned Monte Carlo simulations are exploratory tools for analyzing portfolio sensitivity to regime shifts. They do not predict future regimes and do not constitute financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.
